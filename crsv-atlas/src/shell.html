About this version

Every paragraph was located in its UN document and checked verbatim. Twelve inherited references pointed at the wrong paragraph, four records carried the wrong country or date, and one quotation had no source; all are recorded rather than silently fixed. Coding decisions are provisional pending review by the project lead.
